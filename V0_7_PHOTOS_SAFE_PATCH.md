# AB Kitchen V0.7: verified photo assets

10 exact-match Egyptian food photographs from Wikimedia Commons were downloaded via GitHub Actions, visually reviewed for their dish correspondence, resized to WebP for offline use, and added as bundled image assets. Attribution (creator/license/original link) is visible under Settings > Photo sources.

19 Egyptian recipes previously had illustrative AI visuals. Total Egyptian recipe photos after this patch: **29 of 123**; **94 still missing**. Earlier 104 missing were verified by inspecting the V0.6 sources. The 14 scraped candidates included 4 images excluded from app packaging due to weak visual match/quality. This release does not claim to have solved the full missing-photo task.

All existing recipes, user data, and earlier photos remain untouched. This is a Safe Patch. The CC BY/CC BY-SA licensed photos remain under their corresponding licenses, with credit and link, including WebP format-change notes.
